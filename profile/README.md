**VaxLedger** is a blockchain-based system designed to ensure transparency, security, and reliability in the vaccine supply chain. The platform leverages the **Ethereum blockchain** to provide an immutable and tamper-proof ledger for recording vaccine-related data, combined with **IoT temperature monitoring** using ESP32 powered sensors to guarantee cold chain integrity.

Throughout the supply chain, from production and storage to transportation and distribution, ESP32-based IoT devices continuously monitor temperature conditions and transmit real-time data to the system. This data is cryptographically linked to the Ethereum blockchain via smart contracts, ensuring data authenticity, traceability, and non-repudiation.

Smart contracts automate the validation of temperature thresholds and supply chain events, enabling immediate detection of anomalies such as temperature excursions. Once recorded on the blockchain, all data becomes verifiable by authorized stakeholders, including manufacturers, logistics providers, healthcare institutions, and regulatory authorities.

By combining **Ethereum’s decentralized trust model** with **IoT-based environmental sensing**, VaxLedger enhances supply chain visibility, reduces the risk of vaccine spoilage, prevents data manipulation, and increases overall trust in vaccine distribution processes.

VaxLedger represents a scalable and interoperable solution for modern vaccine logistics, supporting compliance, auditability, and data-driven decision-making in global healthcare supply chains.
